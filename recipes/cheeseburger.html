Cheese Burger

Ingredients

  • Ground Beef
  • Onions
  • American Cheese
  • Bun
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Form beef into patty
  2. Set grill to 350 degrees
  3. press patty onto grill
  4. Flip patty and place cheese
  5. Take off grill when done
  6. place patty on bottom bun
  7. Place lettuce then top bun
  8. Enjoy!

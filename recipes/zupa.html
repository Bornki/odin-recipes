Zupa Toscana

An absolute delicious dish with a spicy kick pretaining of potatos, kale, sausage, and a nice creamy base.

Ingredients

  • Potatos
  • Chicken stock
  • Sausage
  • Diced Onions
  • Kale
  • Heavy Cream

Steps

  1. Dice onions and potatos
  2. Saute onions
  3. put in sausage and split it up
  4. After cooked, put in potatos
  5. Let it cook for 30 minutes
  6. After done, put in kale and cream
  7. Enjoy!
